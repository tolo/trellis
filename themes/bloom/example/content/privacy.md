## The short version

Your notes are **yours**. They live as plain Markdown files on your own device.
We can't read them, we don't sell them, and we never train anything on them.

## What we collect

When you use Bloom without an account, we collect **nothing**. No telemetry, no
analytics, no phone-home.

If you opt into encrypted sync, we store an end-to-end encrypted blob so your
devices can share notes. We hold the ciphertext; only your devices hold the key.

| Data | Stored where | Who can read it |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| Notes (no sync) | Your device | You |
| Notes (sync on) | Our servers, encrypted | Your devices only |
| Billing email | Payment processor | Us, for receipts |

## Your rights

- **Export everything** in one click, any time, as Markdown.
- **Delete your account** and every encrypted blob we hold.
- **Ask us anything** — we answer plainly, no dark patterns.
